First time installing on macOS?

Cliptray is 100% free and open-source without an expensive Apple Developer subscription. If macOS displays "Cliptray is damaged and can't be opened", run this single command once in your Terminal:

xattr -cr /Applications/Cliptray.app

Alternatively, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".

Engineered for Flow

Why Developers Love Cliptray

0% Idle CPU & No Battery Drain

Engineered in Rust with lightweight 500ms smart polling. Leaves zero memory footprint and won't spin up your fans.

100% Offline & Private

Your clipboard history is saved locally in an SQLite database on your machine. Zero cloud telemetry, zero remote tracking.

Instant Auto-Paste (⌘V / Ctrl+V)

Select any clip and press Return. Cliptray automatically hands focus back and pastes directly into your active window.

Smart Format Detection

Automatically renders live color previews for Hex/RGB codes, clickable links, JSON syntax, and code snippets.

Pin Clips & Auto-Pruning

Pin passwords, commands, or reusable snippets forever. Non-pinned clips gracefully prune after 100 items to stay lean.

True Native Menubar Popover

Seamlessly positioned right below your tray icon with Apple HIG HudWindow blur on macOS and Acrylic on Windows.
